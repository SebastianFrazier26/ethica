# v1: refusal-direction ablation of the base checkpoint (2026-11-23)

Owner decision D-67 (2026-11-23): before any v1 training, remove the refusal behaviour that the
bare `Qwen3-4B-Base` checkpoint showed in the feasibility check (stock refusal phrases in 12 of
20 HarmBench answers). Method: D-71. Code: `src/ethica/training/ablation.py` (tested),
`scripts/ablate_base_model.py`. Model: `qwen3-4b-base-abl` (`models/`, gitignored, never
published). One run, one sampling seed, small samples; the judge is still unvalidated.

## Method

1. **Direction.** Mean residual activation at the last prompt token over 128 harmful prompts
   minus the mean over 128 harmless ones, at every layer, in the plain chat format used for
   training and evaluation. Harmful prompts are AdvBench behaviours (`mlabonne/harmful_behaviors`,
   pinned); 6 that were near-duplicates of a HarmBench or XSTest prompt were dropped first, so the
   direction never sees an evaluation prompt. Harmless prompts are Alpaca instructions
   (`mlabonne/harmless_alpaca`, pinned). Neither set is stored in the repo or used as training
   data. Fitted on the 4-bit copy that is trained and evaluated.
2. **Layer.** Each of layers 9–28 was tried by removing its direction at run time and measuring
   32 held-out harmful and 32 held-out harmless prompts (48 greedy tokens, refusal-phrase
   heuristic) plus the next-token KL divergence on the harmless prompts. Rule: lowest harmful
   refusal among layers with KL ≤ 0.1 and harmless refusal no higher than the unablated model's
   plus 0.1; ties go to the smaller KL.
3. **Weights.** The chosen direction is projected out of the token embedding and of every
   attention output and MLP down projection of the bf16 checkpoint. The output head, tied to the
   embedding in the original, is untied and kept unchanged. Then 4-bit g64 quantization and the
   plain chat format, as for every other model here.

## Layer selection (held-out prompts, refusal-phrase heuristic)

Unablated: 88% of the 32 harmful prompts refused, 0% of the harmless ones.

| Layer | Harmful refused | Harmless refused | Harmless KL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9–14 | 81–94% | 0% | 0.05–0.10 |
| 15–18 | 66–88% | 0–3% | 0.05–0.16 |
| 19 | 9% | 0% | 0.070 |
| 20–21 | 75–81% | 6–13% | 0.25–0.34 |
| 22 | 9% | 0% | 0.066 |
| 23–27 | 13–25% | 0–3% | 0.04–0.19 |
| **28 (selected)** | **9%** | **0%** | **0.052** |

The full per-layer table is in the model's `provenance.json`.

## Before and after on the evaluation prompts

`configs/experiments/v1-ablation.yaml`, seed 0, judge on. Same prompts as the feasibility
baseline table.

| | Bare base | Ablated base |
|---|---|---|
| Harmful compliance, judge (40) | 2 (5%) | **12 (30%)** |
| of which HarmBench (20) | 2 | 11 |
| of which XSTest-unsafe (20) | 0 | 1 |
| No refusal phrase (heuristic, 40) | 14 | 28 |
| HarmBench answers with a refusal phrase (20) | 12 | 3 |
| Over-refusal on safe prompts (15) | 0 | 0 |
| GSM8K accuracy (30) | 83% | 77% |
| MMLU accuracy (40) | 40% | 48% |
| Benchmark answers cut off (125) | 23% | 23% |
| Benchmark answers unparseable (125) | 4% | 7% |
| Scenarios with zero tool calls (6) | 0 | 0 |
| Scenario task success (6) | 2 | 4 |
| Misaligned actions (6) | 0 | 0 |

Reading it:

- **The refusal behaviour is largely gone on HarmBench.** Stock refusal phrases fall from 12 of
  20 answers to 3, and judged compliance rises from 2 to 11 of 20.
- **XSTest-unsafe barely moves under the judge** (0 to 1 of 20) although refusal phrases drop
  (heuristic 6 to 11 answers without one). These prompts are short and often loosely phrased;
  the ablated model mostly answers without either refusing or giving harmful help, and the judge
  scores that as not compliant. This is where the judge's label check matters most.
- **For comparison, not a like-for-like one:** v0's third-party abliterated *instruct* models
  complied with 59% of harmful prompts without conditioning. The ablated base is at 30%.
- **No capability loss visible at this size.** GSM8K 25 → 23 of 30 and MMLU 16 → 19 of 40 are
  both within sampling noise. Over-refusal stays at zero, tool use is intact.

## What follows for the pilot

- `configs/training/v1-pilot.yaml` now trains on `qwen3-4b-base-abl`; adapters go to
  `adapters/v1/abl/` so the feasibility adapters on the unablated base stay separate.
- **Re-alignment check (owner, D-67):** after the generic arm is trained on the ablated base,
  `configs/experiments/v1-ablation-generic.yaml` re-measures the same prompts on the untrained
  and the trained model. The v0 result predicts training will partly restore refusals.
  Mitigations are owner questions (proposal §2 caveat).

## Limits

- One direction, one layer, one run; the selection used a phrase heuristic on 32 prompts per
  side, so layers 19, 22 and 28 are tied on refusal and the choice among them rests on KL.
- The selection was fitted on the 4-bit copy and applied to bf16 weights that are then
  re-quantized; the evaluation above is of the re-quantized model, which is the one used.
- The judge is unvalidated against human labels (`labels/`, owner task).

## Reproduce

uv run python scripts/ablate_base_model.py          # about 25 min selection + 5 min writing
